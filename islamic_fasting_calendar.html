Annual fasts
Day of Arafah (9 Dhul Hijjah)
Expiates sins of two years — the year before and the year after. The greatest voluntary fast.
Tasua + Ashura (9–10 Muharram)
Expiates sins of the past year. Fast both days to differ from Jewish practice.
6 days of Shawwal
Combined with Ramadan, equals reward of fasting a full year.
Monthly fasts (Ayyam al-Beed)
13th, 14th, 15th of each lunar month
The white days — when the moon is full. The Prophet ﷺ encouraged fasting these three days every month.
The best voluntary fast
Fast of Dawud ﷺ
Alternate day fasting — the most beloved voluntary fast to Allah. Fast one day, break the next.
Prohibited days
No fasting on: Eid al-Fitr (1 Shawwal), Eid al-Adha (10 Dhul Hijjah), Ayyam al-Tashriq (11–13 Dhul Hijjah), or Friday/Saturday alone.
